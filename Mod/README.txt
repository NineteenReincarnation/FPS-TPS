FPS & TPS 1.0.0 — anno
Mod ID: anno.fpsandtps
RimWorld 1.7 / Harmony 1.7 required. No DLC required.

安装：将 FPS&TPS 文件夹放入 RimWorld/Mods，启用 Harmony 和 FPS & TPS，重启游戏。
推荐顺序：Harmony → Core/已购买的 DLC → FPS & TPS。
设置：选项 → Mod 设置 → FPS & TPS。
不需要启用开发者模式。支持中文和英文。不写入存档，可随时添加或移除。
如果安装了其他 FPS/TPS 显示 Mod，请关闭重复的计数器。

Install: extract the FPS&TPS folder into RimWorld/Mods, enable Harmony and FPS & TPS,
and restart RimWorld. Recommended load order: Harmony, Core/DLC, FPS & TPS.
Settings: Options > Mod settings > FPS & TPS.
Developer mode is not needed. Safe to add/remove from existing saves.
Disable other FPS/TPS overlays if they produce duplicate counters.

TPS is measured from actual completed ticks over real elapsed seconds.
The number in parentheses is the current target. A dash means a new sample is warming up.
FPS uses the game's existing smoothed frame timing. Timing details are ms/f and ms/t.
Only RimWorld 1.7 is supported; 1.6 uses a different runtime.
